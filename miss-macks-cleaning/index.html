Services

What you can ask for

These are the kinds of help Miss Mack's Cleaning offers. Every home is different, so what a visit covers is confirmed when you request an estimate. Gold boxes are sample outlines for this draft. They are not Miss Mack's checklist.

Recurring cleaning

A regular clean on a rhythm you and Maile agree on, such as weekly, every other week, or another pace that fits the home. People use this when they want the ordinary reset handled on a steady schedule. How often, and what each visit includes, is part of the estimate.

Sample Sample outline, not a confirmed checklist

Sample outline, not a confirmed checklist. A finished page might name rooms like this:

  • Kitchen surfaces, the outside of the stove, and the sink
  • Bathrooms, mirrors, and floors
  • Dusting of reachable surfaces
  • A schedule such as weekly or every other week, chosen with you

Deep clean

A more thorough clean than a regular visit, for a home that needs extra attention. People often ask for this before they start a recurring schedule, after a busy stretch, or when a space has been waiting. The scope is agreed before the visit.

Sample Sample outline, not a confirmed checklist

Sample outline, not a confirmed checklist. Extra attention might be described like this, and only if Maile agrees it belongs:

  • Buildup in kitchens and baths beyond a regular visit
  • Baseboards, doors, and inside empty cabinets if that is requested
  • Inside the oven or fridge only when both of you add it on purpose

Move-in and move-out

Cleaning when someone is moving into a home or leaving one. Useful for homeowners, renters, and people handing over keys. Timing and what the visit covers are set when you request the estimate, not assumed from this page.

Sample Sample outline, not a confirmed checklist

Sample outline, not a confirmed checklist. A move clean is often talked about like this:

  • Empty rooms, kitchens, and bathrooms
  • Inside cabinets and the fridge when they are empty and you ask for that
  • Not a hauling or junk-removal service, unless Maile says otherwise later

Organizing

Help putting a space back in order, on its own or along with a clean. You name the area. How much sorting is included is worked out in the estimate, so the size of the job is clear before anyone starts.

Sample Sample outline, not a confirmed menu

Sample outline, not a confirmed menu. Organizing might later be described by area:

  • A closet, a pantry, a room, or another space you name
  • Sorting with you, rather than discarding things without you
  • One area at a time, so the job has a clear end

Laundry and folding

Laundry help and folding for a household that wants that part handled too. Whether it happens during a cleaning visit or on its own, and how it is done in your home, is confirmed with you.

Sample Sample outline, not a confirmed process

Sample outline, not a confirmed process. A later version might say:

  • Wash, dry, and fold using the machines in the home
  • Folded laundry left where you ask
  • Pickup and drop-off only if Maile offers it. That is not confirmed.

Airbnb turnover

Cleaning between guest stays for a short-term rental. Maile already does this kind of work. Hosts can call or email to talk through timing and what the turnover should include. This page does not hold a checkout window.

Sample Sample outline, not a confirmed turnover list

Sample outline, not a confirmed turnover list. Hosts often ask about items like these:

  • Kitchen, baths, and floors reset between guests
  • Linens and restocking only if you and Maile agree they are included
  • A checkout time talked through per stay, not booked on this page
