The problem

When you're in session, who answers?

Your groups run Monday through Sunday, many in the evening. During those 90 minutes your phone can't be your priority, and it shouldn't be. But the person calling may have just gotten a court order, a referral from their attorney, or finally found the courage to ask for help. If they reach voicemail, many won't call back.

What's in place today

What we noticed

Many doors, one person

People reach you by phone, email, your contact form, your client portal, Psychology Today, and TherapyFinder. Every one of those lands back on you to sort, so one warm first contact helps each person get to the right next step.

A program with clear steps

DVIT needs an assessment, then orientation on Tuesdays, then a weekly group. Walking callers through those steps is just what a front desk does well, so they show up ready.

Referral partners already send people your way

Your program serves court-ordered and self-referred participants. When courts, probation, and attorneys know their referrals get a quick answer, they keep sending people.

The usual fix is hiring help

When the phone and the groups both grow, most practices think about a receptionist. Further down, you can see how that compares.

Featured plays

Two plays built for You By Us

Reputation, the respectful way

Willing past clients and referral partners get a simple, no-pressure way to share their experience, so people searching for help can trust you before they call.

Ethics first: nothing is ever sent that pressures a client or reveals that someone was in care. Asks go only to people who've said yes, you approve every message, and we follow your professional code and HIPAA. Many counseling codes discourage asking current clients for testimonials, so we lean on referral partners and peers.

Referral partners

Courts, attorneys, probation officers, other counselors, and community organizations get a quick, warm response every time they send someone, so they know You By Us is easy to refer to.

This is professional-to-professional. Clients are never asked to recruit anyone, and there are no gift cards or rewards.
