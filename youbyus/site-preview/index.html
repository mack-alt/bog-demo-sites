Hi, I'm Lynn J Gardner-Roberts and I am your Balanced Living Coach.

Because I spent a lifetime believing I was not valued, wanted or worthy I attracted neglect, poverty, and stress into my life.  Everyone faces challenges in their lives that may cause stress, concerns, or behaviors that lead to a desire for change. In these times of transition it can be helpful to have a guide who is not a family member or friend; someone who can help by offering insight, tools, & help to create concrete goals to move toward positive change. My intent is to facilitate that process. I believe in addressing the “whole person” mentally, physically, emotionally & spiritually, in ways that fit you & your needs because it's You, by Us.

The ability to manifest what you want is a skillset, it can be learned, and it can be mastered.  Using an integrative approach of individual counseling, group therapy, and workshops we guide you through a mental, emotional, and physical healing process by helping you discover yourself, determine your desired outcomes, and create balance in your life.

Your Vision, Your Life, Guided by Us is THE ultimate self-care plan.

When you live in your truth and within your values you will experience positivity, financial flexibility, and improved wellbeing.

We all could benefit from someone to guide us to our defined destination of success.

Through YouByUs we aim to provide you with transformational services, workshops and a community of like-minded persons who just want to be at their best while becoming their best.

  • Understanding the Assessment Process

At YouByUs Professional Services, assessments are designed to provide a comprehensive understanding of an individual's needs, strengths, challenges, and recommendations for future services. Whether you are seeking services voluntarily or have been referred by a court, attorney, probation officer, employer, or another agency, our assessment process provides objective, professional, and individualized recommendations.

Assessments are clinical evaluations and are not designed to determine guilt, innocence, custody outcomes, or legal decisions. Rather, they provide information to help individuals, referral sources, and service providers make informed decisions regarding treatment, intervention, and support services.

What to Expect During the Assessment Process

  • Step 1: Intake and Documentation

Prior to your appointment, you will be asked to:

  • Complete intake paperwork
  • Sign required consent forms and releases of information
  • Provide relevant court, legal, medical, or treatment records
  • Complete payment arrangements
  • Step 2: Clinical Interview

A qualified professional will conduct a structured interview to gather information regarding your history, current concerns, strengths, and treatment needs.

  • Step 3: Review of Available Information

When appropriate, we may review:

  • Court documents
  • Police reports
  • Treatment records
  • Medical records
  • Other relevant information
  • Step 4: Analysis and Recommendations

The evaluator reviews all available information and develops individualized recommendations based on clinical judgment, professional standards, and applicable Washington State requirements.

Assessment Timeline

Standard assessment completion timelines are generally up to thirty-five (35) calendar days after all required documentation, releases, payment arrangements, and interviews have been completed.

Domestic Violence Intervention Treatment$50 Per Session · 90 min/session

YouByUS Professional Services is a Washington state Domestic Violence Intervention Treatment (DVIT) certified agency that offers assessments and treatment and is designed for both court-ordered and self-referred participants. This 6-12 month program meets weekly for 90 minutes via Zoom, providing a supportive environment for individuals to address their beliefs and behaviors that have led to relationship challenges and resulted in Intimate Partner Violence (IPV) or Domestic Violence.

The primary goal of DVIT is to increase victim safety, promote accountability of those accused of domestic violence and assist in creating lasting behavioral change by helping participants:

Recognize their patterns of power and control in intimate relationships.

Take full responsibility for their abusive behaviors without blame-shifting, minimizing, or justification.

Develop non-violent, respectful, and equitable relationship skills.

Address the belief systems and entitlement attitudes that drive abusive behaviors.

Commit to creating safety for their intimate partners, children, and future relationships.

Participants enrolled with YouByUs Professional Services will engage in lessons that cover the below listed topics as directed by the Washington State Domestic Violence Intervention Treatment program:

Behavioral & Cognitive Change:

Identifying types of abuse, defense mechanisms, cognitive distortions, and indicators of violence to address criminogenic needs and personal motivations.

Accountability & Legal Obligations:

Developing personal accountability, understanding belief systems, and fulfilling financial and legal obligations.

Healthy Relationships & Empathy:

Building respectful relationships, understanding the impact of domestic violence on children, exploring relationship history, and developing empathy.

Self-Regulation & Support:

Establishing healthy self-care practices and building positive community support systems.

Our goal is to help you create a healthier and safer future for yourself and your relationships.

Understanding Emotions. Building Skills. Creating Change.

At YouByUs Professional Services, we believe that emotions are not the problem—how we respond to them is what matters.

Our Anger & Emotional Management Program is designed to help individuals develop healthier ways of understanding, expressing, and managing emotions such as anger, frustration, shame, anxiety, hurt, disappointment, and resentment. Participants learn practical tools to improve emotional regulation, communication, decision-making, and relationships while reducing harmful reactions and conflict.

What is Emotional Management?

Emotional management is the ability to recognize, understand, and respond to emotions in healthy and productive ways.

Many people were never taught how to identify emotions, communicate needs effectively, or manage stress and conflict. Instead, emotions may be expressed through anger, withdrawal, defensiveness, aggression, impulsive decisions, or unhealthy coping strategies.

Our program helps participants:

  • Increase self-awareness
  • Identify emotional triggers
  • Improve emotional regulation skills
  • Strengthen communication and relationship skills
  • Reduce reactive behaviors
  • Develop healthier coping strategies
  • Improve problem-solving abilities
  • Increase personal accountability
  • Build healthier relationships at home, work, and in the community
  • Understanding Anger

Anger is a normal human emotion.

Anger itself is not violence, abuse, or loss of control. It is often a signal that something feels unfair, threatening, hurtful, frustrating, or important. The challenge is not having anger—it is learning how to respond to anger in ways that are safe, respectful, and effective.

Participants learn how to:

  • Recognize early warning signs of anger
  • Understand the anger cycle
  • Identify thoughts and beliefs that contribute to emotional escalation
  • Develop anger control plans
  • Practice healthy expression of emotions
  • Respond rather than react
