What changes

The Reviews Engine

After every great clean, every customer gets asked for a Google review with one simple link. Your front desk sends the ask and a follow-up, and you reply to every review, so everyone knows how great you are.

Done by Google's rules: we ask every customer (no gating), offer nothing in exchange for reviews, never ask for 5 stars, take no reviews from staff or family, and never write reviews for customers.

The Referral Engine

Happy clients share a THA Gift Card with friends. When the friend redeems it, the client gets a THA Gift Card too. Your front desk tracks every referral. The referral reward is completely separate from reviews.
