# Research checklist: one per prospect (never invent anything)

Copy this block for each prospect. A field you can't verify stays **blank**. Blank fields drop out of the page, and the page never shows made-up data.

```
Prospect: ______________________   slug: ______________
[ ] business_name: exact name from website header or Google Business Profile (GBP)
[ ] owner_name (first): from the site's About/Our Story page, GBP "owner" replies to reviews ("- Maria, owner"),
    LinkedIn, or state business registry. Source URL: ______________
    -> If you can't confirm it, the page is NOT ready. Only use --draft for internal preview.
[ ] city: GBP address / service area, or site footer. Source: ______________
[ ] website_label: their domain
[ ] gift_card_name: "<Short name> Gift Card" (no $ amount, ever)
[ ] stats (each one checked): Google rating + count (GBP), Yelp rating + count, years in business (site), awards
    (e.g. Nextdoor Fave) only if shown on their site or profile. Record the date you checked: ________
[ ] Reviews (3-5). For each:
    - Open the review on Google Maps (Reviews tab, Sort: Newest/Most relevant), Yelp, or the site's testimonials
    - COPY-PASTE the text. Don't retype it, fix spelling, or merge two reviews. Excerpts are fine, but cut only at sentence boundaries
    - Reviewer display name exactly as shown (e.g. "Kristen X.")
    - source_url = the link you can share (GBP "Share review" link, Yelp review permalink, or the site page that shows it)
    - Pick the B-O-G service it supports: answer every call / online booking / Reviews Engine / Referral Engine /
      recurring plans / win-backs / quote follow-up / more homes per week / newsletter & events
    - Prefer reviews that mention trust, communication, scheduling, referrals, or long tenure
[ ] What we noticed (2-4): how leads come in (form/phone/online booking?), whether reviews are shown on the site,
    recurring plans offered, first-clean offers (describe them without $ amounts). Facts from their site only
[ ] What we heard (0-3): ONLY things the owner actually said to Kenny. Leave blank for cold outreach
[ ] Final pass: no competitor names, no pricing, and none of the banned words. Generator exits 0.
    Open the page on desktop + mobile and check that every review link opens the quoted review.
```
